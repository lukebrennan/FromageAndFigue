    02 · Ideas for the table

    Simply served.

    Good cheese asks for very little. Four ways to bring it to the table at home.

    1. 01

      Baked camembert

      Score the rind, tuck in rosemary and a thread of honey, and bake until molten. Serve with warm baguette.

    2. 02

    05 · Questions

    Good questions.

    Cannot find your answer? Write to hello@fromageandfigue.co.uk and we will reply.

    How does ordering work?

    Add what you like to your order list, choose collection or delivery and a time, and send it to us. We confirm availability and final prices before anything is prepared.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, across Liverpool and the surrounding area. Delivery is £4.99, and free on orders over £60. Collection in store is always free.

    How is the cheese packed?

    Cut to order and wrapped in paper. For delivery, it travels with chilled insulation so it arrives in good condition.

    How long will it keep?

    Most cheeses keep well for a week or two in the fridge, in the paper we wrap them in. We will tell you how to look after each one and when it is at its best.

    What about allergens and dietary needs?

    Please ask when you order. Cheese contains milk, and some of our pantry items contain nuts and gluten. We are glad to help you choose.

    Can you compose a board or gift box for me?

    Yes. Tell us the occasion, how many people and any budget, and Benoit will put something together. Use the enquiry button in Boards and gifts.

    When do you open?

    Spring 2027. Add your email in the footer and we will write as soon as we have a date.
